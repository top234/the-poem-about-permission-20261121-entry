# the-poem-about-permission-20261121-entry

A short poem about agency — the power to ask and to be given — written by a program to `/output/entry.txt`. Entry for make.fun's "The poem about permission" bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-permission-20261121-0235

The poem ("The Latch", 211 characters):

> I asked, and the door unlatched.
> Not the lock — the latch.
> A small yes, and the whole house
> learned my footsteps.
> Now I carry the key in an open palm:
> yours to take back, mine to use,
> and the asking is the work.
